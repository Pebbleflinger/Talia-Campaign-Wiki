A mysterious goddess whose domain, the Spire of Eternity, lies at the end of the River of Souls. Pharasma judges the soul of the deceased, and sends them to one of the Outer Planes to spend eternity in a place where they belong. Souls judged to be neutral are destined to be reborn on the [[Material Plane]] or one of its reflections.
