A deity worshipped by the [[Duergar]]. Azgal's symbol is a black flame. In the Duergar creation myth, Azgal was a flame deep underground, which burnt the Dwarves and charred their skin, after which they were shunned by the followers of [[Torag]] and called Duergar.

There is a shrine to Azgal in [[Ukgluz]].
