As [[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]] disappears inside the **[[Sibriex]]** lair (?), [[Emilio Astray|Emilio]] turns around and sees [[Aviana Winterwing|Aviana]] appear, with one hand around [[Plex]]' neck and the other hand holding… a screaming goat, making a lot of bubbles. Shalkoc sticks his head through the opening at the base of the spike, and signals that the inside is dry. We follow him in. Aviana asks about the goat, arguing that it was going to be a sacrifice. [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]] rolls his eyes, and promptly roasts the goat with a **Fire Bolt**; "we don't have time for _another_ goat." He pushes me through the opening; I collide into Shalkoc who smelled the roasted goat and wonders what is cooking.

We eventually all slip through the opening, in the residual smell of soggy goat curry. The tunnel we are in is damp, uncomfortably warm, walls glistening and pulsating. It smells like a constitution save… we hold our noses in disgust, but Plex and Emilio don't look so good. We hear an androgynous, gentle and caring voice in our minds telling us: _I felt your arrival, like soft whispers in the æther. Fear not the crossing; you are safe now within this hallowed realm._ Emilio is very angry at this. Something about enough voices in his head…? I tell the party to not trust it, that the demon is trying to get us to let our guard down. Fearghas agrees, and hands me a vial of **silvery powder**… He does not say what it is, only urges me to consume it as he hands a similar one to Emilio.

Aviana explains that Plex acquired a goat hat, and the curry goat that Fearghas murdered. She also says that she and Plex could not save the kittens in the [[Feywild]] from the [[Redcap|Redcaps]], and that we have to return there to save the kittens. Fearghas hands the [[Null-steel chain]] to Aviana, who goes on a tirade about kings and why we should all settle for bondage instead. Plex pulls out his thinking cap, a **Headband of Intellect** in the shape of a goat. Aviana grabs the headband with the plan to reforge it into thinking glasses.

Fearghas asks me whether the Sibriex has mental powers. I say yes, though I can't be entirely certain. He uses a [[spellgem]] of **Mind Blank** on Aviana. Plex no longer detects her with **Detect Thoughts**. She is no longer a thinking creature as far as his spell is concerned… He then recasts **Rary's Telepathic Bond** to include Aviana and Plex into the connection. Plex casts **Fey Step**, ignoring Fearghas' warning against teleporting in here… He takes a step up… and thinks to us that there are enemies next to him. We can't see or hear him. I summon my **Umbral Spirit**, [[Fade]], and tell her to find the Goblin. I also heal Emilio to cure the poisoning incurred when he stepped into the lair.

Aviana steps up to me and says proudly "Look! I can do magic too!" She says some gibberish words, then casts **Death Ward** on me! Fearghas is incredibly proud. Plex is being attacked by humanoid creatures that… look like they used to be people. Some bits swapped, a messed up jaw, and psionic shullbit. Fearghas considers breaking through a wall to get to Plex, then evaluates the thickness of the wall, and reconsiders. He sees something up ahead, and is in turn seen by it too. It's a swarm of maggots, and something invisible. I miss my mum and dad…

Shalkoc runs past Fearghas into the left branch of the tunnel. He sneezes on the swarm of maggots, like only a [[Dragonborn]] can, burning them all like popcorn. Delicious… Something lashes back at him, and he is stunned. Fade runs down the tunnel, attracting unwanted attention. She does not find Plex, and blends into the shadows, turning invisible. Plex casts **Ashardalon's Stride** to unleash fire on the maggots, but a wild magic surge turns him tiny. He picks up popped, not burnt, [[Plex' popped maggots|maggots]].

Aviana congratulates Emilio on being the most sensible, least aggressive member of this party. Plex glimpses an honest-to-gods ABOMINATION, bloated with a second head that is nothing but a gaping maw. I very frighteningly sidle close but not too close to a big lump of wall-flesh… Bits of it seems to be chewing… I don't want to know on what… I inhale the silvery powder Fearghas gave me, trying all the while to look everywhere at once. Aviana chugs a **Potion of Growth** and becomes big again.

Fade is hit by a psychic ray from a Walleye, but the charm has no effect. Plex is getting attacked by something **bitey** but he's so small he dodges… once. On the second bite, he is **swallowed** by the Bloater. Plex uses his **Gift of the Gem Dragon** to push it back, inflicting minor damage. He is still inside the thing, swimming in stomach acid. Fade runs back to where I was to report, as I don't share her senses… but I do feel what Plex is feeling and it's not fun.

Plex blasts the Bloater from the inside with his magic, hard enough to be spit out. Emilio buffs himself somewhat uselessly, but who knows what the future may bring? He joins Shalkoc by a pile of dead maggots. The Bloater dashes towards Aviana, this waddling thing is as big as Aviana. It's aptly named… it's so full of fluid, the skin is tight and it makes sloshing sounds. Disgusted, I focus my magic at it and fire a **Guiding Bolt**! It hits real hard, and I leave Aviana to deal with it as I join Shalkoc, Emilio, and Fearghas. The latter is grumbling at something and flailing with his axe. A wild magic surge makes him explode-y.

Aviana is highly disgusted by the Bloater and dreads getting vomit on her new dress. She enters **Rage** and slashes at the Bloater with her talons, tearing it open. Fluids spill all over her. She is deeply disgusted. I don't blame her. Plex is considering the benefits of being eaten again, even though he is already in a rough shape after the first time he was snacked on. He is bitten by the second Bloater, swallowed again, and uses a MYTHIC FEATURE to convince himSELF that he is not blinded in there. I'm screaming in the telepathic bond because I feel his wounds, and he will die if he is exposed to the stomach acid again. He beholds the wonders of the inside of the Bloater's stomach with eyes open. If we did not need the telepathic bond for a petty thing like SURVIVAL, I would nope out.

Fearghas enquires on the state of the Bloater. Aviana says it's probably almost dead; I squeak that it is still very much moving despite leaking fluids everywhere. Fearghas sends a barrage of **Magic Missiles** towards it. This is how he figures out that there are two of them; because Plex is broadcasting the inside of a Bloater through the telepathic link, and no impacts happen. Shalkoc is punching another monster (the one with a very altered jaw; Obscuroth). As he kills it, Shalkoc hears a voice in his mind again. Almost motherly, it says: _I sense your confusion, and it is alright. Many souls arrive still carrying the shadows of conflict. But that creature… it was just another soul, like you, perhaps a little lost in its own way. Let us try a gentler path to understanding._ When he shares the message into the telepathic bond, I feel sick… and increasingly angry.

Fade runs towards the slashed Bloater and leaps out of the shadows to bite it, before fading into the darkness once more. Plex barely survives being digested by using his **Abjurer's Bangle**. He throws an immovable spear into the Bloater's stomach, then gets out by using **Fey Step**. Emilio blasts maggots and another invisible Obscuroth. He then also hears something in his mind and shares it to us: _Please, children, there is no need for such harshness in this place of healing. Let understanding be your weapon, not violence._ Healing?! I'm going to MURDER this thing! Emilio blasts the Obscuroth all the harder; it responds with a Psionic Rebuke, but Emilio saves.

I slap a **Chill Touch** on the Bloater, but it's not enough to kill it. I back off just before Aviana slashes it again; it collapses and explodes in a burst of acid. Aviana goes down the right-hand tunnel and finds… a cloud of magical darkness. She decides to CHARGE INTO IT. She hits a flesh wall, and attacks the darkness. A Walleye spots her, and fires a Psychic Ray at her. She resists the charming, and continues into the darkness. Plex is calling out in the telepathic bond… none of us knows where he is. I'm sticking with my friends and letting Fade scout.

One of the Obscuroths attacks Emilio, breaking invisibility. He reacts with a **Hellish Rebuke**, frying it where it stands. Shalkoc… catches… a Psychic Ray… and tries to slap a Bloater, to no avail. He dashes, slaps again, but his hand only meets a wall with a disappointing wet sound. Fade takes some acid damage, shakes herself to get rid of the acid, and runs up almost to Aviana. There is a Walleye there, it casts **Darkness**, nobody can see Aviana anymore. There is also an Obscuroth there, but Aviana scares the wits out of it. That's how we figure out it still had wits of a sort. Knowing that does not help in the slightest, because it indicates that there is maybe a person enduring in the monsters…

Plex casts **Maelstrom**, trapping Shalkoc in it while the Bloater escapes the spell almost unscathed… The Dragonborn exclaims "What the hell? I'm coming to save you!" I call out to Plex and try to find him, but in my haste I run right into the line of sight of a Walleye… The Psychic Ray hurts, but I resist the charm. I asked Emilio to stay close to me, he confirms he will follow. Aviana shouts out to me, but she is not in the telepathic bond and I'm too far to hear. She swaps her Spirit Beast's Blessing to Tiger. She pokes a Walleye, then grabs it. She jabs her thumbnail into the eye, making it lose concentration. The darkness disappears but Aviana still can't see a thing.

Another Psychic Ray shoots out of the wall; I ignore the pain, barely keeping concentration, and resist the charm with all my might. Fearghas tries to find Aviana; why did we split up agaaaaaain?! He blasts an Obscuroth with a **Ray of Frost**, then laughs at it magically. "You've got a face like a burst kebab!" He says, dealing psychic damage. Shalkoc takes damage from Plex' Maelstrom. We take care of one another in this party… Shalkoc delivers a bonk on the Bloater with enough force to stun it, then punches it a lot more for good measure.

Fearghas points out an enemy for Fade. She crits her bite, but doesn't manage to kill it, and fades (haha) into the shadows again. The invisible monster hits her hard in response; she is very close to dissolving into darkness. Plex drinks one repulsive potion to heal himself (it's snot), then casts **Sapping Sting** on the Bloater. I step right up to the edge of the Maelstrom and throw my +2 dagger at the Bloater. It hits! The monster still stands, barely. Aviana squeezes the Walleye she is grappling, piercing it with her talons. It's still not dead, so she jumps in place, causing one massive blast from her **Thunderfeet Footwraps**. It hits her too, and she yanks the eyeball out of the wall.

Fearghas once more laughs at the invisible Obscuroth, which barely fails to kill it. "[[Naami Jamas|Naami]], your cat's got this," he says and walks away. He finds Aviana, who chucks him the eye she ripped from the wall. Shalkoc is still unable to escape Plex' spell. This is getting embarrassing… Shalkoc goes into the centre of the Maelstrom to punch the Bloater to death. It explodes, but Shalkoc dodges the acid blast. Turns out, Fade hasn't got this. Her bite misses, and the Obscuroth kills her. My poor Fade dissolves into dark mist.

Emilio launches three blasts against the Walleye that'd been hitting me every turn, but it reflects one of them onto me. It hurts… but I can finally see Plex! Heedless of the cost, I extend a hand towards him… the magic takes my blood to fuel a healing spell. I don't care. Plex' wounds are healed, and a voice speaks in my head: _Such selfless acts are the very essence of [[Nirvana]]. You bring joy to this tranquil place with your innate kindness. You understand already_. I am livid… but suspicion begins to sneak in. Is this the demon speaking, or is it something worse?

Aviana runs past me, and attracts the attention of the Walleye. Emilio asks me for permission, but he won't say what for. I yell at him that this is not a time to keep secrets. Fearghas backs me up, but Emilio refuses to speak. I'm very upset with everyone except Fearghas. Emilio is hit by the Walleye, and reacts with a **Hellish Rebuke**. Aviana hears something come towards us… something huge, stomping with meaty sounds. Emilio stammers that it's bigger than Aviana. The monster (Major Malleable) grabs her; she calls Shalkoc to come help. Plex casts a spell of **Detect Thoughts** to try to locate the enemy, but the range is too short. Plex is eating the [[Plex' popped maggots|maggots]]… he convulses and collapses, unconscious. Uncontrolled terror blinds me for a second, my mind flooded in memories and overwhelming dread. Do I even dare healing him? What if…

Emilio blasts the Major. I run to Plex and examine him… he is afflicted by a form of **madness** that will last 8 minutes. A spell of **Calm Emotions** or **Lesser Restoration** can cure this! Just as I figure this out, a [[spellgem]] begins to glow in Plex' pocket, reanimating him. I yell at him as Aviana dares him to eat another maggot. I heal some of my own wounds, but my magic feels weak, my hands are shaking. I don't think using healing magic on Plex would have harmed him… but the dread that overwhelmed my mind for a moment is not entirely gone. It will get worse. I need to get a grip.

Aviana flexes and makes her cloak billow. Only Emilio sees this because of the magical darkness. She grabs the Major and drags it out of the darkness. She jumps with it, landing in a blast of thunder. That monster is not only huge… it's amorphous, with organs that don't belong in their places… I've seen the like before… I have seen… I see Aviana's wounds, and all my fear returns in a rush as the Major attacks her and grapples her. Half of her wounds would kill me.

Fearghas chugs a **Potion of Maximum Power**, and casts **Scorching Ray**. Three powerful rays of flame hit the Major. Shalkoc repeatedly punches the Major, and realises it is completely immune to acid damage. Plex casts a level 7 **Tether Essence** on Shalkoc and Aviana. They willingly fail the save, letting the spell take effect. Emilio pummels the Major with three blasts, exploding in purple clouds. The misshapen creature collapses and dies.

Finally, the chaos ends… for now. Fearghas begins to cast **Leomund's Tiny Hut** as a ritual… and the voice speaks up in our heads again: _Yes, pause and breathe in the tranquillity, dear ones. The transition from the River to the boundless peace of Nirvana can be… intense. Rest is a balm, and I am here to offer comfort and guidance._ Fearghas grumbles that this demon is weird. Aviana casts **Find Traps** and says that the walls are traps, the floor is a trap, the ceiling is a trap… everything around us is a trap of confusion, disorientation, madness, acid, suffocation, poison, infestation with maggots, exhaustion, blindness, and losing the ability to escape.

Aviana requests Plex to bite her. I draw my dagger and threaten her with it, telling her to sit still and let me heal her. The voice tells us: _Please, try to be calm. Such reactions are echoes from the world you left behind. Here, in Nirvana, all spirits are to be cherished and guided with kindness._ I snarl at it to shut up. Shalkoc casts **Cure Wounds** on Aviana, which pleases the voice. Plex bites Aviana. Fearghas starts violently scolding Aviana and Plex, with good reason.

We attempt a short rest, and heal up. Fearghas pulls out his [[Voidborn Explorer's Spellbook]]. Emilio glares at it for a second; his hands twitch and he struggles for a moment, then transforms into his **[[Void Form]]**. The white of his eyes go black and the bandages on his hands burst open, revealing wicked claws. Eldritch energies spark around his claws. Plex freaks out and pushes Emilio away and out of the bubble with his telekinesis, knocking him prone in the bargain. We question him, but he growls that this is his real form, and there are more important matters to take care of.

As Emilio is thrown out of the hut, he sees that the hallway behind us… looks nothing like it used to. Shalkoc begins to cook an eyeball cake for Aviana, with very little concern. Our wizards figure out that **teleportation and planar travel do not work here**. We're trapped.

As we settle down to rest, the voice speaks again: _Rest now, little spirits, you've just navigated the currents of the River of Souls. It's not uncommon to feel a little bewildered upon arrival. All is well here._ I am convinced now that this is not the demon speaking, but a [[Celestial]] from Nirvana. While we sit and rest, the magical darkness moves towards us. The celestial (?) speaks about the light of dawn as the darkness is coming closer.
