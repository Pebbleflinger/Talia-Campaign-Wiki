As we camp outside of **[[Ukgluz]]** and [[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]] wraps up rice balls, [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]] contacts his father with spellgems of **Sending**. As he opens his bag, the thing blurts out "You're the loveliest hag in the coven." His father is alright, though imprisoned, and Fearghas tells him he is trying to find a solution. Family love.

[[Plex]] and [[Aviana Winterwing|Aviana]] argue, grapple with each other, and both disappear. Fearghas sighs heavily, "we do not have time for that". Indeed we do not, but Plex teleporting another person is a new thing. Fearghas asks about my [[Spellward|wards]], I do my best to give an explanation, but leave out exactly how crippled I am without them. It's not a matter of trust, really… call it an expression of hope. We exchange quick information about our respective abilities. I cast **Augury** to consult the omens on our looking for the Matron, the results come up mixed; weal and woe. Well that is ominous.

We float down to Ukgluz in cloud form. It's an industrial place, all rough black stone. Soot from furnaces thicken the air. The city teems with slaves supervised by [[Duergar]]. There are many statues… The city looks very different from above. The walls are heavily guarded by more Duergar with glaives. (something about chaos dwarves and a war hammer, but none of us is using such a heavy weapon, how very odd!)

We float to the hospital, where I am known and hope to find some help, then we make for the morgue to transform out of cloud form without being seen. Fearghas and me are sneaky, but Shalkoc stumbles. In cloud form. How he manages that is beyond me. A Duergar accidentally inhales a whisp of Shalkoc and coughs. I am a horrified little cloud. The Duergar definitely notices a strange cloud, and alerts others. Shalkoc does his very best cloud impression, it seems to convince them as they laugh and walk away.

The coast is now clear, so we /uncloud in the morgue. There are three bodies covered in shrouds, and not much else. Shalkoc wants to disguise himself as a doctor. I say we should probably leave before somebody finds us living people in the morgue, and that the dead won't tell us much. Shalkoc disagrees and uses his little finger (well, **[[Little Finger of Dam'ghur|Dam'ghur's little finger]]**, which is now Shalkoc's, so Shalkoc's Little Finger Formerly of Dam'ghur's) to cast **Speak with Dead** (and that's a surge). Fearghas establishes a telepathic bond between the three of us.

Shalkoc begins to question the three dead bodies.

S: How are you today?

Dead: Cold.

S: Huh must be very cold being dead then?

Dead: Yes.

Shalkoc is enlightened by this and concludes that it must not be very nice to be dead. Fearghas sighs even as Shalkoc continues with the question "Why are you dead?". They reply in turn "work accident", "defending Ukgluz", and "throat slit by a knife-ear".

Fearghas tries to get Shalkoc to ask better questions, especially from the assassination victim.

S: Where?

Dead: The shrine of **[[Azgal, the Ebon Fire|Azgal]]**.

S: How to get there?

Dead: Walk.

And that's his five questions from the assassination victim. Fearghas makes sure nobody is coming our way. I say I worked with **[[Thorkam Burnbleeder]]** and we could look for him. Shalkoc further enquires from the other dead about the way to the shrine, and gets somewhat usable directions.

Fearghas makes Shalkoc and himself **invisible** so they can follow me undetected as I look for Thorkam. A guard approaches me, limping down the hallway with his glaive. He asks me where I come from, I reply I returned in a hurry because I need to speak to the **[[Filrae Vandree|Matron]]**. Something with the guard seems… off. He is hiding something. I try to pry, but do not want to behave suspiciously and let him be on his way.

Shalkoc tries to grab the Duergar, losing his invisibility in the process. (8 strength but he rolls a nat 20, you can't make this shit up). The guard screams. Fearghas screams too, in our heads: RUN! Shalkoc sprouts his wings and flies off.

_The mental image of Shalkoc flying into a random room, and ending up face to face with a MAD old Duergar lady threatening him with a hairbrush yelling SAAAAAAATANA. No this is not personal experience, ha ha._

None of the above happens in game, our Dragonborn escapes and Fearghas pretends very hard to be a potted plant, unseen, unheard. The Duergar attempt to arrest me for "collaborating with the knife-ears". I think to Fearghas: NOW we run. He breaks invisibility, and casts **Dimension Door** to teleport us to the roof of the hospital. Shalkoc is hiding in the morgue. Fearghas calls him to fly up to the roof, he lands saying "WHEW that was scary!". Both of us proceed to tell him off, and we then turn back to cloud form and hover towards the altar (the shrine of **Azgal**).

Fearghas picks up some of the words the Duergar are speaking; something about a **[[Sceptre of Azgal|sceptre]]** being stolen. We hover as clouds and brainstorm. It is not going well. Fearghas does some cloud shenanigans, casts **Comprehend Languages**, and eavesdrops on the priests at the shrine. It turns out that [[King Dimzad|Dimzad]], the king of Ukgluz, is furious that the scepter has been stolen, and in the name of all Duergar, he has declared war on [[House Vandree]]. Dimzad has sworn to execute all of House Vandree, especially after an unsuccessful attempt at dialogue, and an emissary now missing a tongue.

We decide to seek an audience with Dimzad, which may be a terrible idea, but it's better than blindly scouring the [[Underdark]] looking for the Matron. My very important job is to keep Shalkoc from doing anything stupid as Fearghas walks up to the very surprised king's guards and claims (in Common) to have information about the stolen [[Sceptre of Azgal]]. He asks to speak to the king.

Fearghas is allowed to come in, under heavy guard, arms over his head at all times. If he makes the smallest misstep, he will be killed on the spot. Can clouds sweat? This small cloud is sweating so much, it might be raining. The darker cloud that is Shalkoc next to me seems to be sniffing the air in hopes of food smells, but all we pick up is ash, soot, and sulfur. If a cloud could wrestle another cloud, I would. I just frantically think at him to stay next to me.

Dimzad is one massive Duergar, who is intrigued that Fearghas is not an emissary, and working against the [[King Gandogar|king of Lessia]]. He demands to know how Fearghas knows that Matron Vandree stole an artifact. Fearghas is struggling to explain himself, being very careful with his words. He claims he heard rumours, and reveals that the "Matron Vandree" the Duergar are hunting is the exiled twin sister who is my boss. The king grumbles about surface dwellers not knowing honour; **an oath must be kept no matter what**.

Dimzad asks Fearghas what he has to offer. Fearghas replies "a Halfling who was enslaved by the [[Drow]]". I think "haha, yeah" at that, and Fearghas mentally lifts an eyebrow at me. The king wants to imprison me in the deepest dungeon, and is surprised when Fearghas refuses, and says I am under his protection. The king does not want to let me run free (supposedly I am too useful), but Fearghas, bless his heart, insists that I should be free. Dimzad wants to put me in chains, use me as bait, fire on the Matron (and me) when she comes, and if I live, enslave me to work in the hospital again. Fearghas says it's too obvious a trap and it won't work because of my magic, and Dimzad casually says he can put me in **[[Null-steel chain|anti-magic chains]]**. I start to scream in panic, in Fearghas' head (and Shalkoc's).

The king asks what artifact was stolen from [[Lessia]]. Fearghas honestly replies "it's **the eye of a dragon**". The entire room GASPS (even the walls I am sure; they tend to have ears so why not a mouth too, really). Dimzad is _very_ interested. He asks Fearghas _why_ he wants the [[Dahak's Left Eye|left eye of Dahak]]. Fearghas says he wants to inspect it in the hope to cure a curse. Dimzad is way too interested in the eye (and in the Halfling, that is me, supposedly the head doctor at the hospital spoke highly of me). All of the Duergar are deeply confused as to the simple _notion_ of letting a healer run free…

The king does not believe that Fearghas and his group could deal with the Matron. (I don't blame him honestly.) For us to prove our strength, he tasks us with removing (he says the word "close") a nearby hindrance… a **portal to the [[Void]]**. If we can manage this, Dimzad will help us. (Shalkoc is trying to puzzle out how that "help" will fit into his dragon hoard.) Fearghas agrees to deal with the portal in exchange for the Duergar's help to deal with the Matron.

We discuss the void portal. Fearghas assumes it connects to the Far Realm, and reveals that Plex:

- "acquired" a [[Plex' Demilich|Demilich]]
    
- found a void portal and started to communicate with it
    
- was requested to make a sacrifice to the portal, and chucked the Demilich into it
    
- asked for a friend in return for giving up the Demilich
    
- was promised a friend, but the party closed the portal
    

I have no words.

Fearghas wants to go fish Aviana out of the [[Feywild]] so that we can go deal with the void portal. I suggest scouting and researching first, because picking up our Harpy from the Feywild is going to take a lot of magic. The portal is VERY close by, just an hour's walk from Ukgluz. We float there as fart clouds to investigate. As we travel, Fearghas and Shalkoc fill me in on how they are BOTH contracted to [[Devil|devils]] of [[Hell]].

Fearghas assures me that I do not have to help him, or Shalkoc, fulfil their contracts. But I saved Fearghas' life, and I may be a strong healer, but I am not strong enough to watch a man I've healed, who has helped me, dive into deadly danger. I also admit my other reason to stick with him; if we are fighting [[Demon|demons]], I am IN.

We arrive to an encampment of Duergar that are keeping watch on the Void portal. We have proof that King Dimzad sent us here; a hat he gave to Fearghas, similar to the hat the king is wearing, but much smaller. We /uncloud at the bottom of stairs, to the sound of very heavy boulders being rolled. Fearghas reluctantly puts the hat on. There are ballistae around the camp, pointing at the… stairs? As we approach, the Duergar turn around, look at us, and Fearghas casts **Comprehend Languages**. The Duergar say in [[Deep Speech]] "The unbelievers have come for us!". In the distance, heavy steps approach…

We roll initiative. The camp is also full of large, rolling stone creatures (Granite Juggernauts). I spring into action first, and try to restrain the nearest one with **Binding Shadows**, but they resist my magic. The eyes on these things are burning, but also blacker than night, a darkness so deep it seems to shine. (I snap at Aerelia at this, of course.) My spell has failed, and I very bravely hide behind Shalkoc, who rushes to the thing and punches it very hard. Shalkoc tanks like a boss with **Turtle Style**, but also punches the Juggernaut some more, for good measure. The Juggernaut charges Fearghas, but our Dwarf is too tough for the monster, stopping its charge. (bonk)

One of the ballistae shoots at me, but I am too smol! As Fearghas is distracted by the stone construct strangely… nuzzling… him, an Iron Golem stomps up the stairs and comes to nuzzle Fearghas on his other side. He is deeply uncomfortable. I get shot by another ballista, successfully (and painfully) this time. A chain is attached to the bolt and it drags me across the floor as I (bravely) yell "eeeeeeeeeeeeeeeelp". The same happens to Fearghas, with considerably less squeaking.

The next ballista shoots an explosive bolt at us, Fearghas has sick dodges and I am just out of range (phew). Fearghas summons a **Spiritual Weapon** to hit one of the Juggernauts; the wheels slip out from under it and it crashes to the floor. It would be very funny if the Iron Golem wasn't looking at me like I'm kindling in the middle of winter… I free myself from the ballista bolt and try to run, but it has higher reach than I thought and slices me on the way out. I quickly heal myself but it only begins to knit my wounds.

Shalkoc bravely turtles to the golem and ~~spits~~ breathes on it. It does not work out great. One of the Duergar went invisible at some point so eeeeh… we don't know where he is. Shalkoc gets a major surge that makes all our potions useless. The Golem punches him hard, then turns around and slices me with his sword (natural 20 aaaaaaa). It hurts a lot, and I am badly injured. Shalkoc punches half-heartedly, making very little progress. Fearghas gets harpooned again. The Duergar are running around repairing the ballistae. One of the Duergar tries to run, falls down, and breaks his neck. Less work for us…?

The bag of scolding savagely roasts Fearghas. He slaps a **Curse of Biting Cold** on the Iron Golem; Fearghas makes sure to shout to Shalkoc about the effects of that curse. He then shoots all of five Scorching Rays at the closest Juggernaut (pew pew). He teleports himself out of the ballista bind like a boss, and bravely takes cover behind a pillar.

I'm hurting, bleeding, and afraid… I intended for a healing spell on myself, but for a moment the shadows guide my hand, and I direct them to the golem and the closest Juggernauts for a **Shadow Monsters** spell. It works! I take cover behind the Golem, cast a **Healing Word**, and then watch in awe as Shalkoc punches the hell out of the Golem… with his fire gauntlets… which would normally heal it. I crouch (bravely of course) behind the golem, and the next ballista shot misses me.

Fearghas magically and bravely shoots at everything. Go Fearghas! The Juggernaut I hit with Shadow Monsters just rolls over the iron golem, absolutely flattening it. I frantically heal myself, popping sorcery points like candy. I want to take cover but I got hit and grappled by the braglista (the bragging ballista that insists I am a tiny fairy) so there is no way to wriggle free just now. Shalkoc goes to ~~spit~~ breathe COLD on the juggernaut and the very flattened iron golem. He also punches really hard again. What a lizard!

Meanwhile I am still getting repeatedly shot like I'm a plump little duck. Through multiple severe piercing wounds, I grit my teeth and hold on to my concentration with all my might. Fearghas tells me to get closer, that they shoot at the farthest target. Fearghas shoots at things once more, and tries to be the target for the bolts. I'm bleeding heavily; in barely controlled panic, I stumble to Shalkoc, free myself from the grappling bolt, and remove my wards to heal myself. It drains me, but the pain begins to fade.

Shalkoc rushes in, but as a deep stab wound opens on his body, a Duergar with a glaive appears next to him, **Invisibility** spell shattered. My wards are on the floor and my body feels slow… I need to get to Shalkoc! The golem spews poison at Shalkoc, who appears completely unaffected (all the same, I squeak a little bit). Fearghas begins to rapid fire at the ballistae once more, getting frustrated at how poorly they burn (despite being made of wood). _Aerelia is a little sheepy because he gave them lots of HP._

Shalkoc is barely standing in the middle of enemies. I take out my **[[Onyx Dog Figurine]]** and throw it to the floor, yelling the command word AMBULANCE! Dwen the Rock appears, I hop on his back and we run towards Shalkoc. My spellwards all but forgotten, I reach out to apply a _juicy_ Healing Word on him (7th level). It takes a heavy toll, but Dwen steadies me.

The Duergar hits Shalkoc, and he is enlightened by this. "Nah," our Dragonborn says with a dominating smile, "let me show you how it's done." He can now crack a whip as well as Jill. Who's Jill? Don't worry about it. He takes an attack like a champ to punch back; the Duergar does not get back up. Fearghas is hanging out at the back, protected by a **Shield** pierced by dozens of ballista bolts. He tries again to cast **Fire Bolt**, fails again as his armour hampers him. He is fiercely grumbling.

I move in, still riding Dwen, and fire (haha) a **Cone of Cold** from my staff, and heal myself… almost to full… because my max HP has been lowered so much. Shalkoc continues punching, invigorated by my healing. Fearghas grits his teeth as he dashes and loads up a **Ray of Frost** that finally goes through, and deals a number on the golem, but it still stands. He commands his spectral axe to slash at the golem, getting it square in the chest. A black sapphire tumbles out! I limp to pick it up.

We made it… Shalkoc and me are injured and I'm shaking from the channelling fatigue, but Fearghas is mostly grumbling with GREAT conviction due to his armour's arcane failure rate.
