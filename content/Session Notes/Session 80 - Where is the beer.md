**Thursday 30th** **Nonus, 1497**

**17:55**

Fearghas entrusts the **[[Naami Jamas#Wand of Catnap|Wand of Catnap]]** to me. It can make up to 7 willing creatures take a short rest in only 10 minutes, basically. We must wait for Saturday because we first need a long rest to recover high-level spell slots. We immediately start discussing ways to bypass the invitation (sneaking, forging). I don't think that's a good idea…

Fearghas casts **Legend Lore** as a ritual, because he can apparently? He investigates the 3rd level of Heaven, **[[Heaven#Venya|Venya]]**. It's colder than the other levels, snowy mountains. There are cities in valleys connected by roads and bridges. This layer focuses on craftsmanship and community. **[[Torag]]**'s realm is located there. It's much less defended than the 1st and 2nd level… logically. Any dangers there are mainly natural, like avalanches. Nobody expects ~~the Spanish Inquisition~~ an attack on this level.

Aviana decides to buy a lot of chocolate/confectionaries, sneak it into Shalkoc's **Snack Pack** (Bag of Holding but for food), and bring it to the angels as a show of kindness. Shalkoc considers eating up all the treats, then offering himself to the celestials. We go talk to the Elder of Promise, a priest of **[[Sarenrae]]** called **Gavel**. He gives us a very exact definition of Lawful Good. We discuss whether a Divination spell like **Augury** would fail Kyrin's contract. Depending on the wording, it very much could.
