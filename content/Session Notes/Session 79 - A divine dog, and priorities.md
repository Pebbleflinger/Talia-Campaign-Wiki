**Wednesday 29th Nonus, 1497***

**18:55**

Kyrin and me return to the cabin after spending time conversing with **[[Kali]]**. I tried to communicate with spiders, no luck yet. Emilio ~~hatches~~ wakes up from his power nap. Kyrin asks where **[[Kaar]]** is. We hear him trampling around on the floor above. I wonder whether Aviana is teaching Kaar to speak too, but we hear growling and a Harpy screech. "Clean your paws before you step on my clothes!" Fearghas' temple vein starts to pulse as he conspicuously looks away from the ceiling. He is still under the effect of a surge that causes him to track dirt everywhere he goes.

Some random older woman from [[Promise]] comes by, walking a rat-sized dog. Shalkoc, thanks to an earlier surge, gets on his knees, eyes wide, saying he has never seen anything as holy as this dog. Shalkoc tries to prompt a religious conversation about the dog being the saviour of all, while the old lady stammers the dog **Hufflepuff** is her little darling. Shalkoc wants to steal the lady's dog, but the sweet lady gets terrified by Ivernia who is casually flying in. The dragon makes a beeline for the dog, looking forward to a bite-sized snack. Shalkoc steps in and picks up the dog; she shivers in his hands like she's about to straight up die from fear. The old lady passes out from sheer terror.

I try to convince Shalkoc to let the dog live its life. He locks Ivernia out and begins feeding and worshipping his new god. Fearghas tiredly asks me where Shalkoc got the dog. Kaar is barking, Aviana is shouting, and Ivernia flies up and makes another dive for the dog. Fearghas tries to get Shalkoc to give up the dog. He is unwilling. I rush to the aid of the dog's owner who is still lying in a heap close to the cabin. Emilio tries to bait Ivernia with treats to get her away from Shalkoc's newfound deity. He fails to get Ivernia's attention, but _by god_ did he get the dog's attention, and she is going for Emilio with all her tiny might. Kyrin is giggling her bum off, I don't blame her, I'd do the same if I wasn't trying to revive the poor lady.

- he no longer sees the doggie as a god
    
- he loses his 3rd-level spell slot
    
- he loses his -1 to constitution
    
- he loses his +1 to intelligence

Fearghas grumbles at Kyrin because she could have helped instead of giggling herself half to death.

I manage to wake the old lady up, explain what is going on, and apologise for Shalkoc's behaviour. Fearghas shows up with the dog dangling from his finger. The old lady yanks the dog from Fearghas' finger and invites us over for cake. Her name is **Rose**. Very sweet. She returns to Promise, insisting we come over for cake. Fearghas sees he cannot keep me away from cake, but I suggest we go **without** Shalkoc.

Fearghas and me return to the cabin where Shalkoc is feeding treats to Ivernia. She is behaving, likely due to being very hungry. Emilio is flabbergasted. I ask him, first time? This amuses Fearghas. We sit down like civilised people, and Kyrin talks about our conversation with Kali. We get a lot of extra information on the [[Drow]] sisters **[[Filrae Vandree|Filrae]]** (my boss who was exiled) and **[[Minae Vandree|Minae]]** (the sister who is now at the head of [[House Vandree]]).

Kali mentioned one **Velkyn Arkenaii**, a friend of her mother's who fled the city **[[Ul'ath'tallar]]**, where House Vandree has great influence. Kali hasn't seen Velkyn in over 50 years, but supposes he might be in [[Aethel'eren]] (which means "noble lands" in Elvish). Or more likely on one of the surrounding isles, which we know next to nothing about. That's far away, not that it matters a lot to us, I'm sure it's beautiful country, we'll add this to the long, long list of our quests. ~~Nagaaaaaaaaaaai.~~

We do need to report to [[Zariel]] first, or we might be toast. Literally.

The problem is, Fearghas can't teleport us to [[Hell]] until Saturday. We could ask Zariel to teleport us there, but since we don't really have much to report, she probably won't take kindly to it. I am not an angel slayer, dang it Shalkoc! We consider casually flying Promise all the way to Aethel'eren but it will take a while. Fearghas wants to wait for Kali to rescue his parents. Kali mentioned she had connections in [[Taldor]]; we might be able to employ scouts from there to go to [[Ildilas]] and investigate the situation!

Kali goes off to [[Lessia]] to scout. Kyrin goes to bother Aviana and Kaar because FLUFFY. Kali returns around midnight, alone. She left a letter for Fearghas' parents so they know they are being rescued. Kyrin asks about her connections in Taldor; I mention the need for scouts to Ildilas. Fearghas brews up **Potions of Supreme Healing**. He advises Shalkoc to ask around in Promise for craftsmen.

Fearghas blows up at Plex about the mushroom pit in front of the door. I offer to help him relocate the mushroom pit so it's not in the way, while Fearghas stomps off. Kyrin considers cleaning her **[[Kyrin Niteshade#Sun Blade|Sun Blade]]** just to mess up the Light-Shy mushrooms… While us small people are gardening, the grown-ups, plus Kyrin, are having serious talk with Kali about adventurers to hire. I join and outline three profiles of people I want as scouts:

- a sailor with knowledge about the weather

It would be great to have a person capable of flight, but I'm not counting on it. At first I add I don't want the scouts to endanger themselves, but Kali, Fearghas, and Emilio talk me down. If the adventurers take the job, they are aware of the risks and know they may die. I can't consider myself responsible for protecting them. I can only promise to try… Fearghas growls about messing up his alchemy. We discuss hiring someone to gather herbs for him to ~~screw up~~ brew more potions. Most of us go to bed. Kyrin wants to stay up and wait for Kali, as she isn't tired. Plex goes to the kitchen, curls up in a big pot, and goes to sleep. Fearghas is rage-sleeping.
