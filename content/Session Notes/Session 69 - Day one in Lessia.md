[[Naami Jamas|]]
I meet the heroes at the gate. They are very kind to me, [[Aviana Winterwing|Aviana]] is a lot bigger than I expected! She nearly tramples some of the king's guards by mistake. Apparently this is temporary; [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]] gave her a lot of non-soup potion?

The undead army threatening **[[Lessia]]** was scattered, so the king is preparing to throw a big party. There will probably not be any lemoncakes, but I still want to go.

Fearghas reports to the advisor to the [[King Gandogar|king of Lessia]] (the king is nowhere to be seen) that the party delved into the **mausoleum of [[Thamdarul]]** to remove the undead general. The advisor nearly swallows his tongue at the news, and requests proof. He pulls a corpse from his bag of holding, as evidence. It's the corpse of **[[Drakar Zul]]**, the undead general and servant of the **[[Vrazzak|Lich]]**.

The advisor fails to find words, asks Fearghas to put away the body, and goes to find the king. This is clearly far above his pay grade. A ring of Dwarven guards surrounds the door the advisor leaves through; they are very serious about their job.

The advisor, **[[Mordram]]**, returns with the king, who remains behind an honour guard of 30 Dwarves. He thanks Fearghas for getting rid of the undead general, and announces the feast will also be dedicated to the party.

We leave the king's hall, and a giant Aviana waves at us. As if she was not TOWERING above everyone present. She proceeds to apologise for the rudeness. She has been speaking with the captain of the guard, **[[Torgrus]]**. He appears very tired, for some reason…

[[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]] is ravenous, and Aviana goes on about her glasses. Fearghas is pleased by the outcome, somehow. Aviana thought I was a child, due to my very small size. I am offended. She is simply too tall!

As Mordram said quite clearly that Fearghas smells (he indeed does), **[[Linzi the bard|Linzi]]** recommends walking to a clear lake outside the gates to take a bath. Shalkoc is unwilling, and would rather kidnap a glassblower. Nobody wants to let Aviana stomp to the lake alone.

We all go to the lake. Shalkoc jumps into the cold water, Aviana opens a bottle, and Fearghas begins to scream. Shalkoc then gets out of the water, in a sense, and walks on it to the shore. Aviana says this is how he swims. Fearghas admits he doesn't know how the big lizard is doing it.

It turns out, Aviana freed an invisible **Haunt** from the [[Aviana Winterwing#Iron Flask|Iron Flask]] and we are all flailing trying to catch it. I use my wand to cast **Detect Magic**, it does not help at all. We eventually have to give up and accept it flied away. Fearghas has a lot of words with Aviana, none of them friendly.

Shalkoc picks up a skeletal finger and begins to wave it around. I can sense necromantic energy in it; he casts **False Life** on himself and is very pleased with it. Linzi is happily taking notes, claiming this book will sell like crazy. I can see it. I can feel Fearghas' nerve weakening.

We walk back to Lessia and Fearghas explains about the giant rock floating in the sky. This is their island, **[[Promise]]**. I struggle to comprehend all of this…

The feast begins, and the king makes a speech. At this moment, a telepathic connection opens in my mind, and my boss, the **[[Drow]] [[Filrae Vandree|matron Vandree]]**, tells me to explain why the undead army was routed, what is going on in Lessia, and what I've heard. I report with as much information as I am able, fearing for my life, my heart sinking into my socks as I am betraying Fearghas' home even as he sits right beside me. I ask whether the Drow will attack now, and before I can get a reply, the connection ends. I blink and come back to my senses, feeling sick. The king is nattering on about the people of Lessia and their courage, his gratitude to Fearghas… I am considering excusing myself, but I do not dare.

Fearghas whispers to me that the king is corrupted by a dragon's eye! The captain of the guard comes to Fearghas and whispers that a Dwarf is here to see him and it is urgent. Fearghas gets up to leave, and I leap on the excuse to go with him, to leave the throne room. We meet with **[[Barrik]]**, who reports to Fearghas about the situation with the [[Duergar]].

Lessia's mines are very close to **[[Ukgluz]]**, the Duergar settlement I've come from… For years there has been an agreement between Lessia and the Duergar that they do not mine closer to one another. Recently however, orders have come from the king of Lessia to dig the other way. He MUST have known he would find the Duergar. Two or three weeks of digging found the Duergar, and the incident happened. We suppose the dragon eye is what caused the king to grow aggressive and seek to expand his territory.

As we discuss this, we hear a hollow roar coming from just above us! (I squeaked out of character, everything is fine.) A command rings out in everyone's minds; "Give back what was stolen!" We rush out to see a massive skeletal dragon carrying an undead man with a staff.

Fearghas and Aviana **Windwalk** to the gates, and Shalkoc sprouts a pair of wings, grabs me despite my screaming, and leaps into the air. 300 feet up above Lessia, I feel his grip slipping a little, and start apologising to my mummy and daddy. Somehow, he does not drop me.

We immediately start fighting the two undead figures. The dragon has a sickening bilious aura, and the **Skull Lord** teleports them both, it bites me because I am small (the audacity!), inflicting acid damage. We struggle, Shalkoc punches, the dragon starts destroying Fearghas. He is bleeding and within an inch of his life! Heedless of the risks, I rip my wards from my wrists and channel a heightened spell. I cast **Heal at 9th level**, leaving Fearghas with barely a small cut.

Aviana tackles the dragon and drags it underwater, but it still beams its necrotic breath at us, Aviana dodges most of it but it hits me with full force. Fearghas shoots the Skull Lord several times with a **Scorching Ray**. Aviana is teaching the dragon to swim, for some reason.

We defeat the Skull Lord, and the **Black Deathdragon**, by shooting Shalkoc and having him deflect the missiles onto the dragon. Aviana emerges grumbling about poke-eyyyy-mons, and chucks the corpse of the dragon onto the shore so that Fearghas can start chopping it up.

Aviana carries the corpse of the dragon into Lessia, which is swarming with guards looking for Drow. The king's honour guard is blocking the entrance to the throne room. As we approach, the king leaps out and accuses Fearghas of being in league with the Drow, and having stolen the **[[Dahak's Left Eye|left eye of Dahak]]**! Not just that, but also to have lead the undead army against Lessia!

We tried to reason with the king. We tried everything. He is having none of it, and our only option was to be stripped naked and cast into prison to face "justice"… or resist. Fearghas put an end to the pointless argument, told his king he was ashamed of how low he had sunk, and teleported us all out of the throne room. We arrive in **the Locus on Promise**.
