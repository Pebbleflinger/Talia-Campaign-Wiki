A tall and muscular [[Harpy]] woman who receives the blessings of animal spirits and harnesses her rage to become formidable in battle.

## Key to the World

One of the greatest parts of Aviana's legend is that she broke the Key to the World, a mysterious crystal which kept the [[Dragon Aspect|Dragon Aspects]] (and all other dragons) imprisoned. 

## Iron Flask

A mysterious iron flask in Aviana's keeping. She is known to throw it at creatures to imprison them inside, and also to release them at the worst possible time. The flask has been used on [[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]] and Aviana herself before, and caused them a severe headache.

## Null-steel chain

A chain made of dark metal, like steel covered in soot. It inhibits low-level casting and generally makes spellcasters feel sluggish and unfocused. The [[Duergar]] priests make these chains by using the blood of [[Celestial|angels]]. Every day, Aviana longs to wrap it around Plex.
