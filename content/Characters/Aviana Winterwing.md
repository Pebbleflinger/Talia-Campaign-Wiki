A tall and muscular [[Harpy]] woman who receives the blessings of animal spirits and harnesses her rage to become formidable in battle.

## Iron Flask

A mysterious iron flask in Aviana's keeping. She is known to throw it at creatures to imprison them inside, and also to release them at the worst possible time. The flask has been used on [[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]] and Aviana herself before, and caused them a severe headache.
