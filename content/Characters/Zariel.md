The Archduchess of [[Hell#Avernus|Avernus]], she is a fallen angel who is missing a hand.

On the 30th of Nonus 1497, the party returned to [[Hell]] as Shalkoc wanted to report to Zariel and show off the "angel slayer" he'd found (which was [[Naami Jamas|Naami]]). Zariel was predictably unimpressed with a terrified Halfling, and as [[Emilio Astray|Emilio]] tried to defend her, Zariel grabbed him by the throat and threatened to kill him. This had two consequences: Kyrin made another contract with Zariel to place the [[Kyrin Niteshade#Beacon of Doom|Beacon of Doom]] on the third layer of Heaven, and Emilio made it one of his life goals to kill the Archdevil.
