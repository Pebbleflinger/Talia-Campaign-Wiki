A distinguished and monocled Quadron from [[Mechanus]] whom the party met as he was working in the library of [[Heaven]]'s Shore. Claude helped the party with their research for a time, which nearly cost him his existence as he was corrupted while researching the [[Void]]. The corruption could be reversed by way of Aviana whacking him hard enough that he forgot the problematic query (also known as concussive maintenance or the Mending cantrip).
