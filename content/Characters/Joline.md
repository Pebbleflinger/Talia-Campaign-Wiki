A [[Dryad]] who is partially corrupted into a [[Spriggan]]. In early Decimus 1497, the party rescued Joline from [[Muravelara]] and took her to [[Heaven]] where she was healed. The corruption could not be undone, and Joline's appearance and personality have become rougher and fiercer than any Dryad's.

As her beautiful Moonlit Grove in the [[Feywild]] was taken over by The Grey, she has moved to [[Promise]] where she tends the Springseed Forest.
