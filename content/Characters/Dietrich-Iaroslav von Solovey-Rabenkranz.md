The count of [[Solgorod]] in the Grand Duchy of Morovya. Dietrich was turned into a vampire by [[Magdalena Sofiya von Ustevya-Rabenkranz|Magdalena]]. 

The party met Dietrich in late Undecimus 1497. Though they expected an enemy, they found Dietrich to be not only friendly but also surprisingly ethical for a vampire. The party has a mutually beneficial (if slightly uneasy) relationship with Dietrich and invited him to [[Promise]] in Duodecimus 1497.
