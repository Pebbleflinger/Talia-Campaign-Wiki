A smuggler and a friend of [[Sael]]'s. Edgar is involved with some shady types, one of which captained the ship [[Naami Jamas|Naami]] boarded to return to [[Ildilas]], but due to the storm around the island, the ship instead continued to a [[Drow]] trading port south of [[Quadira]] where Naami disembarked. Edgar gave Naami 6 spell scrolls of Sending to keep in touch.
