A [[Halfling]] from the remote island of [[Ildilas]]. She excels in healing magic, but limits her own potential by wearing two bracelets known as [[Spellward|spellwards]].

[[Naami Jamas|Naami]] has severe trauma from being lost in the lair of a [[Sibriex]]. She had been separated from her party of [[Drow]] under [[Filrae Vandree|Matron Vandree]]'s orders, and found herself alone in a chamber with a tortured [[Celestial|angel]]. As she tried to help him, her magic instead caused him to die horribly, and she fled the lair.
