A [[Halfling]] from the remote island of [[Ildilas]]. She excels in healing magic, but limits her own potential by wearing two bracelets known as [[Spellward|spellwards]].

[[Naami Jamas|Naami]] has severe trauma from being lost in the lair of a [[Sibriex]]. She had been separated from her party of [[Drow]] under [[Filrae Vandree|Matron Vandree]]'s orders, and found herself alone in a chamber with a tortured [[Celestial|angel]] (whom she would learn later was Liorel, Clementiel's brother). As she tried to help him, her magic instead caused him to die horribly, and she fled the lair, losing her spellwards.

## Onyx Dog Figurine

A small carving of a dog. Upon saying the command word "ambulance", the figurine turns into a black mastiff. Naami occasionally rides on the dog's back and affectionately calls him Dwen the Ambulance Dog or Dwen the Rock.
