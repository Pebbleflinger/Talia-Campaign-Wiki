A [[Halfling]] from the remote island of [[Ildilas]]. She excels in healing magic, but limits her own potential by wearing two bracelets known as spellwards.

[[Naami Jamas|Naami]] has severe trauma from being lost in the lair of a [[Sibriex]]. She had been separated from her party of [[Drow]] under [[Filrae Vandree|Matron Vandree]]'s orders, and found herself alone in a chamber with a tortured [[Celestial|angel]] (whom she would learn later was Liorel, Clementiel's brother). As she tried to help him, her magic instead caused him to die horribly, and she fled the lair, losing her spellwards.

## Spellwards

Pieces of jewellery commonly worn by the people of [[Ildilas]]. Spellwards (or wards for short) can be anything from bracelets to rings or necklaces, but they are all made of a silvery alloy that slightly inhibits spellcasting. The Ildic culture around spellwards is extensive. Naami's spellwards are two silvery bracelets which were made by her father [[Sael]], one of which has a sooty black stain.

## Piercing the Veil

One of Naami's innate abilities, it allows her to see through darkness (natural and magical) for one minute.

## Onyx Dog Figurine

A small carving of a dog. Upon saying the command word "ambulance", the figurine turns into a black mastiff. Naami occasionally rides on the dog's back and affectionately calls him Dwen the Ambulance Dog or Dwen the Rock.

## Fade

Naami's Umbral Spirit, a shadowy panther brought into being by a spell and a black sapphire. Fade does not speak, but she understands the languages Naami speaks.

## Wand of Catnap

A wand topped with a figurine of a sleeping cat. Naami can use it to sing a little song, give a soothing head pat, and put up to seven willing people to sleep to grant them a short rest in 10 minutes.
