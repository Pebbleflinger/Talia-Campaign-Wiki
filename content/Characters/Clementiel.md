An angel specialised in healing who works as the head of a hospital wing on the first layer of [[Heaven]]. Though he appears young, he usually looks tired.

The first time he met [[Naami Jamas|Naami]], Clementiel scolded her for channelling spells without her wards. He then said he recognised the staff she carries and gave her a formal "Thank you."
