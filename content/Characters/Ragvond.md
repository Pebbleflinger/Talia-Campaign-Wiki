A [[Frost Giant]] who is a general of [[Utgard-Loki]]. Ragvond is a cheerful man who likes the cold, large amounts of alcohol, and a good raid. After the party's first visit to [[Jotunheim]], he has become an ally and same to stay in [[Promise]] in Decimus 1497. On this occasion, he gave the [[Aviana Winterwing#Transdimensional Anchorchain|Transdimensional Anchorchain]] to Aviana. Part of the agreement with the Frost Giants involved large quantities of [[Bali]]'s beer.
