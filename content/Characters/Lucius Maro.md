A vintner who lives in [[Taldor#Taldor Augusta|Taldor Augusta]]. Though he has a mansion in Carinae, he also owns a keep outside the city, which is surrounded by vineyards.

Lucius enjoys power and popularity in Taldor and is close to the Emperor. His true nature is that of an old vampire who sends Taldorian blood to [[Strazhland]]. After [[Kyrin Niteshade|Kyrin]] found out Lucius was responsible for the death of her foster sister, she developed a personal hatred for the vampire and is set on exposing him as a monster and killing him.

While sneaking below Lucius' keep, [[Naami Jamas|Naami]] found a black mask and concluded it was the mark of a leader of the [[Crimson Circle]].

At the same time, Kyrin found evidence that Lucius has been to [[Muravelara]] and is likely consorting with the Hags who follow [[Gyronna]].
