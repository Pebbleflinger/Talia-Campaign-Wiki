The [[Dragon Aspect]] of Might, Dahak the Black.

## Dahak's Left Eye

After Dahak was killed by [[Vrazzak]] and raised as an undead, his left eye was briefly in the keeping of the Dwarves of [[Lessia]] where its influence drove [[King Gandogar]] mad.

Dahak's left eye was then stolen by the [[Drow]] and is in the keeping of [[Filrae Vandree]].

## Dahak's Right Eye

After Dahak was killed by [[Vrazzak]] and raised as an undead, his right eye was kept in [[Khaz Narak]] until it was stolen by the party in Duodecimus 1497.
