The current leader of the [[Shadar-Kai]] in the absence of the [[Raven Queen]]. As the party spent some time in the [[Shadowfell]] after rescuing Nyrissa and two other Shadar-Kai from [[Muravelara]], Lady Aladriss showed a vision of Aurora to Emilio. In exchange, Emilio pledged himself to Lady Aladriss and the [[Raven Queen]] as a Shadow Knight.
