The king of [[Lessia]]. Once a just and fair king, he was corrupted by the evil influence of [[Dahak's Left Eye]] and turned into a tyrant. He was later kidnapped by [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]] and the party, taken to [[Promise]], and exposed to the good influence of [[Dahak's Right Eye]] to reverse the corruption. Though this process has cured the king's madness, the political consequences are not so easily undone.
