A former privateer turned drunkard when his crew mutinied and stole his ship, leaving him stranded on an island. Gregry earnt the affections of the Siren sisters [[Araphia]] and [[Aradeia]] and they helped him return to shore (by tasking a giant crab to carry him).

Gregry harbours vengeful feelings toward his former quartermaster who started the mutiny, [[Bartholomew]]. After meeting with [[Emilio Astray|Emilio]], [[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]], and [[Naami Jamas|Naami]] in Primus 1498, he agreed to sail to [[Ildilas]] on two conditions: that the party provides a Taldorian warship, and that they deliver Bartholomew to Gregry so he can exact his revenge and keelhaul him.

Gregry wears a fancy but threadbare coat, has a peg leg, a hook hand, and an eyepatch. He can drink anyone but Shalkoc under the table. After Emilio, Naami, and Shalkoc returned from the Galleon's Graveyard with his letter of marque and reprisal, Gregry prepared to return to his life at sea.
