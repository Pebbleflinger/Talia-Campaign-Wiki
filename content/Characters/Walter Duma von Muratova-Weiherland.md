The current duke of House Kreuzenberg. He does not attract attention in the Strazhi political scene, but he has ruled Grimmburg for about 400 years. [[Dietrich-Iaroslav von Solovey-Rabenkranz|Count Dietrich]] believes Walter may have the means to become a Grand Duke and could be biding his time. He is following the direct orders of the [[Anabella|White Thirst]] of the [[Crimson Circle]], but does not know it himself as his memory of receiving said orders was altered.
