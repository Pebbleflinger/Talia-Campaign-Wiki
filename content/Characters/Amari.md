A [[Halfling]] who is [[Naami Jamas|Naami]]'s mother. She is Ildic and married to [[Sael]]. A herbalist and physician by trade, Amari chose not to undergo the witch trials and she permanently wears [[Spellward|spellwards]]. She has the aptitude for magic but has not trained in it, and she can at most cast a 1st-level spell.

Amari's personality is fiery and she is fiercely protective of her loved ones. When Naami decided to leave [[Ildilas]], Amari argued at length with her. Eventually Amari gave Naami her blessing to travel, but to prepare her for the dangers of the world, she taught her as much as she could about herbs and healing.
