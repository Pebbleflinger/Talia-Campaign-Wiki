A [[Human]] warlock changed by spending twelve years in the [[Void]], who takes on a monstrous form to fight with his claws.

## Void Form

Emilio's true form. It is closer to that of a [[Voidborn]], though he still looks human. When Emilio transforms, the white of his eyes go black and his claws grow and spark with energy. His voice becomes deeper and rougher, and he can't concentrate on spells. However, he can use powerful boons to further increase his combat abilities.

## Robe of Stars

A powerful garment worn and treasured by Emilio. It was stolen from [[Filrae Vandree]] by [[Lyrielle]], and modified with cat ears on the hood. It allows him, among others, to enter the [[Astral Sea|Astral Plane]].

## Gold chain set with a fire opal

A nonmagical pendant found in the [[Sibriex]] lair. Emilio was fascinated by the beauty of it, and kept it for a long time.

He later gifted the pendant to [[Aradeia]] as an apology for fighting her and her sister. The Siren understood it as him courting her, and she has been smitten by Emilio ever since.
