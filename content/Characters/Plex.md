An elderly [[Goblin]] who is the shaman of his tribe. Plex can control gravity and time, but his true potential lies in his utterly unpredictable creativity.

## The Void Dragon



## Babbling Book of the Befuddled Braniac

A book found in [[Mennon]]'s mansion. The book is sentient but refuses to open until it has seen the play *The Fall of the Gith*, performed by an all-Flumph cast.
## Plex' Chickens

Small wooden statues of chickens, with tiny knife beaks. They can animate and attack. Seven of Plex' chickens were destroyed as the party fought to close the [[Void]] portal close to [[Ukgluz]], and the only survivor was picked up by [[Emilio Astray|Emilio]].
## Plex' popped maggots

A handful of maggots Plex picked up in the [[Sibriex]] lair after [[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]] hit them with a dragon breath. These are popped, not burnt. Additionally, eating one will cause a short bout of severe madness.

Why Plex has not destroyed them is anyone's guess. He's made plans to weaponize them in the past, and probably will do so again.
