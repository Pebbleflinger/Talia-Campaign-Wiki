A Blood Hag who resides in [[Muravelara]]. [[Kyrin Niteshade|Kyrin]] made an unexpected ally of her during one of the party's visits to Mura under the New Moon. As a result, the women of the party are not affected by the Banishment spell placed over the dark swamp.
