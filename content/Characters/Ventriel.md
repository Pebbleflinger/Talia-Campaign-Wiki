A celestial from [[Nirvana]] whom the party met in [[Heaven]]. Ventriel is a very positive artist who paints his wings with magic colours. He is a follower of [[Shelyn]] and says everyone is an artist, most people just don't know it yet.

[[Plex]] was delighted to meet Ventriel and bought a lot of paint off him. Kyrin ordered a custom item from Ventriel, which would become the [[Aviana Winterwing#Painter's Plume|Painter's Plume]].
