A [[Changeling]] from [[Taldor#Taldor Augusta|Taldor Augusta]]. Orphaned at a young age, Kyrin developed the skills of a spy and an assassin. In addition to her psionic powers, she can exert some control over her sentient shadow.

## Portable Hole

A piece of fabric which turns into the opening of a hole when spread on a flat surface. Kyrin keeps food and adventuring supplies in the Portable Hole, but has also used it to carry the rest of the party for very short durations.
