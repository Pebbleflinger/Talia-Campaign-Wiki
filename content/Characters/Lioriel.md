An [[Celestial|angel]] from [[Heaven]], Lioriel was a healer who was reckless in his haste to help people. He heard the desperate prayers of mortals trapped in the lair of a [[Sibriex]] in the [[Underdark]] and went to their aid, but he was captured by the demon too. This is where [[Naami Jamas|Naami]] found him, as he was being tortured and corrupted. In trying to help him, Naami caused his death.

Lioriel's brother in Heaven, [[Clementiel]], regrets not being able to guide him and keep him safe. Naami has searched for Lioriel in Heaven, but his soul has not returned yet.

Through what she can only assume was [[Sarenrae]]'s intervention, Naami inherited the [[Naami Jamas#Staff of Power|Staff of Power]] Lioriel carried. One of his feathers is also in her keeping, and she treasures it despite the painful memories tied to it.
