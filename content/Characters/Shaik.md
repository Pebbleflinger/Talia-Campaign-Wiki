A Marshal of [[Heaven]]. She is a beautiful dark-skinned woman with curly hair, amber eyes, and golden wings. The party sought Shaik's advice on the matter of 2 contracts with [[Zariel]]: Shalkoc's contract to assassinate Shaik, and Kyrin's contract to place the Beacon of Doom on the 3rd layer of Heaven so the devils may invade it. Shaik told the party the only way for Shalkoc and Kyrin to own their souls again was to legally buy them back from Zariel.

Shaik has history with Zariel, and now views her with sadness as "another poor soul". When Zariel fell from Heaven, Shaik went to Asmodeus to ask for Zariel's return, but she was told Zariel had chosen to join forces with the devils.
