A [[Drow]] matron who was exiled, and stole [[Dahak's Left Eye]] from [[Lessia]]. She is a powerful spellcaster, able to cast the Gate spell and travel between planes. She is known to stay in a castle in the [[Astral Sea]].

Formerly the boss of [[Naami Jamas|Naami]] and [[Kyrin Niteshade|Kyrin]].

A Legend Lore spell brings up the words: exile, revenge, ambition.
