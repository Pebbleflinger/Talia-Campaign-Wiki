A red dragon who was born without wings. Imix was going to be killed by [[Girra|Girra the Red]], but he escaped his fate. He now lives in Surtr's Jaws and though the [[Fire Giant|Fire Giants]] worship him like a deity, he only wants peace.

After a long and initially tense conversation with the party, [[Kyrin Niteshade|Kyrin]] formed a bond of friendship with Imix.
