A [[Halfling]] who is [[Naami Jamas|Naami]]'s father. He is not Ildic, and shortens his birth name Safael to Sael to fit in with Ildic name conventions. Sael was a silversmith before settling on [[Ildilas]] and getting married to Amari, and now he uses his skills to make [[Spellward|spellwards]].

Sael is a gentle man who believes in people and destiny more than he believes in gods. He was a traveller and an adventurer in his youth, but does not speak much of that time. A connection he still has from these days is to the smuggler [[Edgar]].
