The Grand Duchess of [[Grand Duchy of Morovya|Morovya]]. She rose to power in recent years, in great part thanks to the magic she learnt from [[Vrazzak]]. Her castle is in [[Krovozamok]]. She is the one who turned [[Dietrich-Iaroslav von Solovey-Rabenkranz|Count Dietrich]] into a vampire.

When the party meets Magdalena, she is obsessing over Dwarves and their history, and she immediately begins questioning [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]]. Later, she also gets an interest in [[Emilio Astray|Emilio]]'s abilities when she finds him unaffected by her Zone of Truth spell.
