A handful of maggots [[Plex]] picked up in the [[Sibriex]] lair after Shalkoc hit them with a dragon breath. They are popped, not burnt. Additionally, eating one will cause a short bout of severe madness.

Why Plex has not destroyed them is anyone's guess. He's made plans to weaponize them, and probably will do so again.
