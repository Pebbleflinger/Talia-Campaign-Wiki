The left eye of the Dragon Aspect of Might, [[Dahak]]. After Dahak was killed by [[Vrazzak]] and raised as an undead, his left eye was briefly in the keeping of the Dwarves of [[Lessia]] where its influence drove [[King Gandogar]] mad.

Dahak's left eye was then stolen by the [[Drow]] and is in the keeping of [[Filrae Vandree]].
