A small carving of a dog, owned by [[Naami Jamas|Naami]]. Upon saying the command word "ambulance", the figurine turns into a black mastiff. Naami occasionally rides on the dog's back and affectionately calls him Dwen the Ambulance Dog or Dwen the Rock.
