A gemstone upon which a spell has been inscribed. A spellgem can be used by anyone independently of their proficiency in spellcasting (or lack thereof). Spellscribing using gemstones is an art jealously guarded by the [[High Elf|High Elves]] of [[Aethel'eren]], but [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]] learnt it from [[Vrazzak]].

A spell inscribed in a spellgem will never cause a wild magic surge.
