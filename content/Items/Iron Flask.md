A mysterious iron flask in [[Aviana Winterwing|Aviana]]'s keeping. She is known to throw it at creatures to imprison them inside, and also to release them at the worst possible time. The flask has been used on [[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]] and [[Aviana Winterwing|Aviana]] herself before, and caused them a severe headache.
