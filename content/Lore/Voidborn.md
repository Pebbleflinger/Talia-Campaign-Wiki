Creatures originating from the [[Void]]. They tend to be inherently mad and act on pure instinct.

Particularly dangerous Voidborn the party have faced include the Star Spawn Seers in combination with the Star Spawn Hulks; the Seers hit the Hulks with their magic, and it is amplified as it reverberates outward from them.
