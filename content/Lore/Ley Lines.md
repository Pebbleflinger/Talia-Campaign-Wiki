Currents that carry newly formed souls from the [[Ethereal Sea]] to the [[Material Plane]]. These new-born souls seek a mortal vessel to manifest in, and when they don't find one in the Material Plane, they are carried on to the [[Feywild]] and the [[Shadowfell]]. Natural gateways to either of these two planes are usually found close to a Ley Line.

Though they are made of magic and not visible to the naked eye, Ley Lines usually follow waterways and take the path of lesser resistance. There are observable signs indicating the presence of Ley Lines.

## Ley Magic

The practice of harnessing Ley Lines to cast spells, recharge a magic item, or regain spell slots. It's a dangerous and taxing process, one practiced by the [[High Elf|High Elves]] of [[Aethel'eren]]. Sea Elves also used Ley Magic to influence the weather, an art they learnt from [[Ishtar|blue dragons]]. Practicing Ley Magic without being properly trained causes channelling fatigue.
