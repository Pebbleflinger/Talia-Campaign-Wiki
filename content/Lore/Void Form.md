[[Emilio Astray|Emilio]]'s true form. It is closer to that of a [[Voidborn]], though he still looks human. When Emilio transforms, the white of his eyes go black and his claws grow and spark with energy. His voice becomes deeper and rougher, and he can't concentrate on spells. However, he can use powerful boons to further increase his combat abilities.
