Tiny gemstones that allow a modification on a spell.

The party found Subtle Mind Crystals, which allows the casting of a spell without verbal or somatic components, in the [[Tower in Jotunheim|tower in Jotunheim]]. Several months later, [[Kyrin Niteshade|Kyrin]] found similar crystals in [[Lucius Maro|Lucius]]' bedroom.
