A desert south of the [[Ashur Plateau]] and [[Quadira]]. The Redwind Sands were once a lush valley home to the [[Yuan-Ti]]. Legends tell of massive building wrought by magic, and of priests capable of transferring a curse from an object to a person. There is an ancient temple to [[Nethys]] in the Redwind Sands.

Though the area is said to be just sand and ruins nowadays, [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]] had a vision of a city there (and of [[Magdalena Sofiya von Ustevya-Rabenkranz|Magdalena]] looking over it). The party has reason to suspect that the Redwind Sands may hold knowledge of necromancy and lichdom, and also that the long-dead civilisation of this area may not be as dead as anticipated.
