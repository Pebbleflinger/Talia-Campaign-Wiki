Mechanus is the plane of perfect and impersonal order. Its only layer Axis is made up of giant gears turning with perfect synchronicity. Clockwork creatures such as Modrons inhabit this plane, and it is also home to the *Bronze Lady*, [[Brigh]], and the *Arbiter of Scales and Streets*, [[Abadar]].

The party travelled to Mechanus on the 7th of Decimus 1497 to seek help with two infernal contracts.

There are no [[Wild Magic|wild magic surges]] in Mechanus.
