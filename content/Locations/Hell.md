A plane also known as the Nine Hells or the Pit, it is shaped by evil and law. Hell is home to devils who cruelly enforce wicked institutions. The hierarchy of Hell is strict, and at its very top sits the Archdevil [[Asmodeus]]. His lieutenants oversee each layer of Hell. Planar travel is only possible from and to the topmost layer Avernus.

## Avernus

The first layer of Hell, it is ruled by the Archdevil [[Zariel]]. Avernus is a desolate landscape of burning heat and meteor showers.

## Black Vents

A wide crater located on Avernus. It is actually a planar wound, a spot where two planes were fused by a Wish spell and were then torn open by the denizens of Hell. Fearghas' research into the Black Vents made mention of the Demon Lord of Monsters and Multitude, Lamashtu.

## Dis

The second layer of Hell, it is an endless city of burning heat dominated by the Iron Tower where the Archdevil [[Dispater]] rules in his paranoia.
