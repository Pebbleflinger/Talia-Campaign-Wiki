A plane also known as the Nine Hells or the Pit, it is shaped by evil and law. Hell is home to devils who cruelly enforce wicked institutions. The hierarchy of Hell is strict, and at its very top sits the Archdevil [[Asmodeus]]. His lieutenants oversee each layer of Hell. Planar travel is only possible from and to the topmost layer [[Avernus]].
