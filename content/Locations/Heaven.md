Also known as the Seven Heavens, it is a plane of goodness shaped by law, and justice tempered with mercy. It appears as one gigantic mountain with a flat summit, where night never falls. Planar travel is only possible to the city of Heaven's Shore on the first layer, Threshold.

Heaven is home to celestials; among which are angels, Devas, and Planetars. These beautiful winged people submit any traveller to a long registration process involving questions about their intentions, and being informed of the laws of Heaven. Registration is conducted in the Administratorum and overseen by Verita, an angel who can see through illusions and hear lies. Each time a traveller comes to be registered in Heaven, six randomly chosen celestials fulfil the role of Witnesses and help in ascertaining the traveller's intentions.

In Heaven's Shore, the party received treatment and healing at the infirmary, more of a hospital really. An angel called Valeriel listened to Fearghas venting his frustration and rage for several hours, mainly about Aviana, Plex, Shalkoc, and goats. An angel called Caelus examined Emilio's hands and gave him a pep talk. An angel called Clementiel lectured Naami about channelling spells without regard for her own health.

## Pilgrim's Path

A trail up Mount Celestia, it is the only way to ascend past the first layer of Heaven. Every walk up the path becomes a journey of self-reflection and faith, where physical distance does not matter as much as the traveller's pilgrimage. The Pilgrim's Path is only open to those bearing an invitation to the higher levels.

## Mercuria

The second layer of Heaven, Mercuria is a place of beautiful rolling plains. It is home to [[Iomedae]] among other deities, and great warriors spend their afterlife here. The party was invited to Mercuria to attend a ceremony in honour of [[Ezeriel]]'s fallen friends.
