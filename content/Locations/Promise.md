A flying island, home to the party. It was formerly part of [[Deadlight]], and is now steered by [[Shalkoc Zornaxc|Shalkoc]] and kept aloft by elementals from the [[Elemental Planes#Plane of Air|Plane of Air]]... but not for much longer.

## Springseed Forest

A forest of beautiful trees close to the town of Promise. The seeds originated on [[Nirvana]] and the trees bear fruit year round. Springseed trees can also awaken as Treants and defend their caretakers. The forest is tended by the Dryad Joline.
