A domain in the [[Abyss]]. It is a dreary swamp, home to Gyronna's Daughters and other Hags. There are only two moon phases in Muravelara, in opposition to the full moon and new moon of the Material Plane.

The party travels to Muravelara on the full moon and new moon with Plex' lantern. On one of these excursions, [[Kyrin Niteshade|Kyrin]] made an ally of the Hag [[Viola Marshlegs]].
