Lessia (not Moria) is a Dwarven hold in the [[Silver Peak Mountains]]. It is home to [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]] and his parents [[Dulum]] and [[Ysolde]].

Lessia is governed by [[King Gandogar]] with the help of an advisor. In Nonus 1497, King Gandogar's advisor was [[Mordram]], but he was branded a traitor and replaced with a Duergar woman from [[Ukgluz]], presumably a priest of Azgal.

In late Nonus 1497, Lessia was besieged by an army of undead commanded by [[Drakar Zul]] under the orders of [[Vrazzak]].

King Gandogar's honour guard is composed of elite warriors in heavy armour and captained by [[Torgrus]].

In Lessia, the party met with [[Linzi the bard]] and [[Barrik]], as well as [[Naami Jamas|Naami]] who then joined the party.
