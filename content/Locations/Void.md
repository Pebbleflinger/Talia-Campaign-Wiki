The plane that lies beyond the [[Astral Sea]]. Also called the Far Realm, very little is known about the Void, as anyone who venture into it are soon corrupted by its madness.

[[Emilio Astray|Emilio]] is the only person known to have survived the Void without losing his mind, though this is understood to be thanks to [[Aurora]]'s power protecting him.
