The plane that lies beyond the [[Astral Sea]]. Also called the Far Realm, very little is known about the Void, as anyone who venture into it are soon corrupted by its madness.

[[Emilio Astray|Emilio]] is the only person known to have survived the Void without losing his mind, though this is understood to be thanks to [[Aurora]]'s power protecting him.

## Voidborn

Creatures originating from the Void. They tend to be inherently mad and act on pure instinct.

Particularly dangerous Voidborn the party have faced include the Star Spawn Seers in combination with the Star Spawn Hulks; the Seers hit the Hulks with their magic, and it is amplified as it reverberates outward from them.

## Deep Speech

A language spoken by denizens of the Void, but also by races who live underwater such as [[Deepfolk]]. It is made up of alien sounds such as clicks and deep trills. People with a weaker mind can be physically or mentally harmed from just hearing Deep Speech.
