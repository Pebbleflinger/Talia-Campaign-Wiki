An archipelago south-east of [[Strazhland]]. It is a very isolated place, difficult to sail to because of hostile weather. However, there is no wind on the Mistborne Isles themselves or the surrounding sea. Their closest diplomatic ties are with the [[Iorian Keys]]. The archipelago may have been an outpost of the fallen Saurian Empire; some books mention Yuan-Ti and Lizardfolk living there. The current inhabitants of the Mistborne Isles are Elves and Humans. It is also home to the only known [[Changeling]] community.

There is a gate to the [[Shadowfell]] on the Mistborne Isles. Some of the Strazhi noble houses have interests in the [[Shadow Gate]]s on the archipelago, such as House Kreuzenberg. This interest lies in taking control of the Shadow Gates to further the goals of the [[Crimson Circle]]. A Strazhi noblewoman in a white mask, presumably the [[Anabella|White Thirst]], directed Count Walter to send more resources to the Mistborne Isles to secure Shadow Gates, thereby putting House Kreuzenberg in a vulnerable position against other factions such as House Eberwald.

For reasons nobody has managed to make sense of yet, [[Plex]] believes something he is looking for is on the Mistborne Isles.
[[Kyrin Niteshade|Kyrin]] is looking to travel there to meet other Changelings.
