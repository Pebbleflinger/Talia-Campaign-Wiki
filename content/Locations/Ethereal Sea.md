A transitive plane which contains the [[Material Plane]] and its reflections, the [[Feywild]] and the [[Shadowfell]]. Positive energy and negative energy collide in the Ethereal Sea, and this is where new souls are created before they are carried away on [[Ley Lines]] to find a vessel to manifest in.
