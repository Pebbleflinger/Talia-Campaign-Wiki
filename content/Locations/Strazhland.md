A country to the south of the [[Mountains of Mourning]]. Strazhland is divided in duchies ruled by vampire lords and ladies. The land itself is always choked in mist, one of the manifestations of the ancient curse of the [[Snow Elf]] gods.

## Grand Duchy of Morovya

One of the 13 Grand Duchies of Strazhland. It is currently ruled by [[Magdalena Sofiya von Ustevya-Rabenkranz|Magdalena]]. The capital city is Krovozamok. The party has an ally there in [[Dietrich-Iaroslav von Solovey-Rabenkranz|Count Dietrich]] in the town of Solgorod.
