An island off the coast of [[Quadira]], south of [[Taldor]]. Ildilas is frequently considered to be part of Quadira, though the Ildic don't share the sentiment. [[Naami Jamas|Naami]] is from Ildilas.

Most of Ildilas is a desert, and its population lives in small clusters at the edges of the island, where sheer mountains provide shade from the burning sun. These mountains form a near-perfect ring around the island, with a break to the north. There, the mountains give way to the greenery of Bottleneck Coast. The largest settlement of Ildilas, also called Bottleneck, sits on this coast.

Ildilas is populated mostly by [[Halfling]]s and [[Human]]s. The Ildic language is a dialect of the Halfling language; it is a rhythmic and archaic language that places great emphasis on syllable count. Though the Halflings of Ildilas are content to stay in their homes and make the best out of the harsh climate, the Humans sometimes venture inland to explore the desert.

In recent times, Ildilas has been inaccessible by any means of transport, magical or otherwise. A storm surrounds the island, and even attempts to teleport there or contact someone on the island by means of a Sending spell fail. After researching the unnatural weather, and speaking of Ildic legends with [[Inas]], Naami concluded the storm is made by a flight of blue dragons... maybe even [[Ishtar]] herself.

Naami's research also turned up the origin of the island itself; Ildilas is the summit of a mountain that once dominated the Sea Elven Empire, now sunken beneath the waves. The Ildic have stories of finding tower-tops buried in the desert.

The Ildic have a unique tradition of sorcery, which they call witchcraft. Though they also have many priests of [[Sarenrae]] and [[Pharasma]], many Ildic spellcasters choose to become witches who tend to focus on healing magic (though they often have other powers). Unlike other forms of sorcery, the source of their powers is not within themselves. Their bloodline carries instead an unusual conductivity to magic. While this conductivity makes spellcasting easier, it puts constant stress on the caster's body. Ildic witches wear [[Spellward|spellwards]] to inhibit their own ability for magic, a practice which has become central to their culture and traditions.

On Ildilas, a child who chooses to become a witch goes through several trials of increasing difficulty. The final tests are finding their way home through the wilderness, and being without their spellwards for an hour.
