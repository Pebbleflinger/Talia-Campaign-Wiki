A realm of fire within [[Limbo]], where [[Fire Giant|Fire Giants]] live.

## Surtr's Jaws

A volcano in Muspelheim. The party teleported there to retrieve a piece of Rovagug's Chains, on [[Utgard-Loki]]'s request. Within the caldera of Surtr's Jaws is a fortress, Obsidian's Cradle, where Fire Giants worship the red dragon [[Imix]].
