Four elemental planes surrounding the Ethereal Sea. Each of these planes represents Water, Air, Fire, and Earth.

## Plane of Air

The home of air elementals, such as the ones keeping [[Promise]] aloft.

## Plane of Water

A world of deep oceans... into which [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]] once accidentally teleported.
