A race of dark-skinned [[Dwarf|Dwarves]] native to the Underdark. The Duergar are generally hostile to surface-dwelling races and harbour resentment towards their light-skinned cousins, though there is no one they despise more than the [[Drow]]. Duergar consider themselves superior to most other people and usually own slaves.
