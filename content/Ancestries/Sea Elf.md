A race of Elves who lived by the sea. The Sea Elven Empire has long sunken under the waves of [[The Great Expanse]]. People of the Sea Elven ancestry are known to live in [[Aethel'eren]], where they have integrated within the High Elven way of life.

Sea Elves learnt magic to manipulate the weather from [[Ishtar|blue dragons]].
