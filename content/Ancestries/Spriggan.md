Spriggans are vengeful forest spirits capable of casting spells. They were once [[Dryad|Dryads]], and their gentle nature was corrupted into a being of tangled branches and claw-like fingers.

The Hags of [[Muravelara]] have been conducting rituals to corrupt Dryads into Spriggans that will serve them.
