Hags are humanoid who look like disfigured elderly women. Depending on where they come from, they appear very different and abilities vary wildly.

## Gyronna's daughters

The first of their kind, nine daughters born of the Hag Mother [[Gyronna]]. They call her Abyssal lair [[Muravelara]] home and further their plans with the help of lanterns such as [[Plex#Shadow Lantern|Plex' lantern]].
