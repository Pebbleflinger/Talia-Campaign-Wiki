A race of towering humanoids who live in volcanoes. Fire does not hurt them.

The Fire Giants living in the volcano known as Surtr's Jaws, located in [[Muspelheim]] within Limbo, worship the red dragon Imix. They are at war with the [[Frost Giant|Frost Giants]] of [[Jotunheim]].
