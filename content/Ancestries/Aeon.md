Not much is known about these creatures of absolute order. They are thought to originate from the [[Astral Sea]] but some dwell in [[Mechanus]]. When Fearghas mentioned an Aeon to [[Shaik]], she grew stern and advised we stay ignorant of them.

Aeons are capable of casting or undoing a Wish spell.

The party has twice dealt with an Aeon; the first time, they were given a task for the promise of a Wish spell. The second time was on the 20th of Undecimus 1497, when [[Fearghas MacAllistar|Fearghas]]' and [[Plex]]' minds were put into each other's body.
